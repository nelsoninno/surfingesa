# surfingesa.com, your website package

Last updated October 2026.

## What's here
This folder is your whole website. `index.html` is the English home page and `es/index.html` is the Spanish one. Your photos and logos live under `assets/images/`, and your colors and fonts live in one file, `assets/css/tokens.css`.

## The videos
Two background videos are already in `assets/video/`: the surfing clip at the top of the page and the aerial ocean clip behind "Mar de Sueños & Waves Well". Each comes in a large (desktop) and small (phone) version. To swap one, keep the same file name, no sound, MP4 (H.264), and keep every file **under 25 MB** (ideally under 8 MB).

## Replace the photos
Drop your originals into `_source/photos-original/` (any format works). The web-ready versions go under `assets/images/`:

| What | Where it appears | Folder | Recommended size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero still / video poster | Top of the page | `assets/images/hero/` | 1920 × 940 px |
| Lessons / Rentals / Business cut-outs | The three cards under the hero | `assets/images/sections/` | same size as the current files |
| Founder photo with the yellow swoosh | "Meet the founder" | `assets/images/sections/` | 1672 × 985 px |
| Community photos | Strip of 5 photos | `assets/images/gallery/` | 600 × 750 px (portrait) |
| Merch photos | "Buy our merch" | `assets/images/merch/` | 1440 × 1090 px (poncho 1440 × 1760) |
| Logos | Header, hero, footer | `assets/images/logos/` | SVG if possible |

## Change your colors
All colors and fonts are in `assets/css/tokens.css`. Editing that one file re-skins the whole site.

## Request a change
Message us on WhatsApp with what you'd like changed. Small text or photo changes are quick. The site will be live at https://surfingesa.com.
